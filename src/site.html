Everything in one app

Two-way translation for Deaf and hearing people, and a full course to learn signing.

📷Live sign → words

The camera reads fingerspelling A–Z (with Ñ), numbers 0–10 and everyday signs like hello, thank you, please, help and I love you.

🔊Read aloud, on or off

SENYAS can say each recognised word out loud in English, Filipino or Bikol. Switch it on or off with one tap, and choose whether letters are spoken too.

🧑‍🦱Words & voice → sign

Type or speak a sentence and the 3D avatar signs it, fingerspelling names and new words. Slow it down to follow.

⠃Braille reader

Upload a photo of braille or scan it live with the camera. SENYAS reads Grade 1 braille (with Ñ), shows the text and can say it aloud. Typed words are shown in braille too.

🎓Lessons that check you

Alphabet, numbers, greetings, family, feelings, questions and emergencies, with quizzes, flashcards, games and camera practice.

🗣️English, Filipino, Bikol

The whole app, every word and the phrasebook come in three languages, with Bikol words from Central Bikol.

✋Teach it your signs

Record any sign three times — a local FSL sign, a name sign — and SENYAS reads it from then on.

🔒Private and offline

Video never leaves your phone. After the first start it works with no signal, which matters in an emergency.

📱Fits every screen

Phones, tablets, laptops and TVs, upright or sideways. On big screens both directions sit side by side as a conversation.

How it works

Start the camera

Hold your hand in view. SENYAS tracks 21 points on each hand, on your device.

Sign

Hold a letter for a moment, or make a word sign. The words appear and, if you like, are read aloud.

Answer back

The hearing person types or speaks, and the avatar signs the reply.

FAQ

Which sign language does SENYAS use?

The one-handed manual alphabet and core everyday signs shared by Filipino Sign Language (FSL) and American Sign Language (ASL). Signs differ by region, so SENYAS lets you teach it your own.

Is my camera recorded?

No. Video is processed on your device and never uploaded or saved. See the privacy policy.

Does it need internet?

Only the first time, to download it. After that it works offline. Voice typing may need internet, depending on your phone.

Can it replace an interpreter?

No. SENYAS helps with everyday talk and learning. For hospitals, courts and emergencies, ask for a qualified FSL interpreter.
