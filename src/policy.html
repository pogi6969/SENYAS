Privacy policy and cookies

Effective 3 November 2026 · applies to the SENYAS website, the SENYAS web app and the SENYAS Android app.

In short: SENYAS reads your hands on your own device. Camera video is never uploaded, recorded or shared. SENYAS has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

1. Camera and photos

When you press Start camera, video is passed to the hand-tracking model running inside your browser or the app. Only the positions of your hand joints are used, in memory, and are thrown away moments later. No picture or video leaves your device. You can stop the camera at any time; it also stops when you leave the screen.

Braille photos you upload, and braille seen by the live scanner, are read on your device in the same way. The picture is not uploaded or kept; only the text you choose to keep appears in your conversation history.

2. Microphone and voice

The microphone is used only while you hold the microphone button, to turn speech into text for the avatar. Speech recognition is provided by your browser or phone (for example Google or Apple), which may process the audio on their servers under their own privacy policies. Reading aloud uses the voices installed on your device.

3. What is saved on your device

If you allow it, SENYAS saves your settings, lesson progress, conversation history, the signs you teach it and your personal examples in your device's local storage (similar to cookies). This stays on your device. You can export it, or delete it any time in Settings → Privacy & data. If you choose Essential only, nothing but that choice is saved and everything resets when you close the app.

4. Cookies

  • senyas_consent (essential, website): remembers your cookie choice for 12 months.
  • senyas_lang (preference, website, only if you accept): remembers the website language for 12 months.
  • senyas.* local storage (app): see section 3.

SENYAS uses no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies.

5. Downloads and hosting

The website is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (such as IP address) for security. If the hand-tracking files are not already on your device, they may be downloaded from jsDelivr or Google's model storage; those services see a normal web request. After the first download, the app works offline.

6. Children

SENYAS is made for learners of all ages, including students. It collects no personal information, so no account or parental data is needed.

7. Your rights

Because SENYAS does not collect your data on any server, there is nothing about you for us to hold, sell or share. Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173) you may still ask questions or raise concerns using the contact below.

8. Sign language accuracy

SENYAS is a learning and communication aid, not a certified interpreter. For medical, legal or emergency situations, ask for a qualified FSL interpreter.

9. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change and the app will ask for your choice again where needed. Contact: open an issue on the SENYAS project page on GitHub.
